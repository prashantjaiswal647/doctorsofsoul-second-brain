## 🎨 Creative Philosophy: Ultra-Low Production Cost, Maximum Stop-Rate
We do not need expensive video shoots or complex 3D renders. High-contrast typography on solid backgrounds achieves:
1. **Lowest CPM (Cost Per 1,000 Impressions):** Meta rewards clean, text-legible, high-CTR images.
2. **Maximum Curiosity:** Disrupts infinite scrolling faster than generic stock photos.
3. **High Intent:** Pre-qualifies readers who actually read and value written wisdom.
